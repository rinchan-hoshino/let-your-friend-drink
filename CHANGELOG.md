# Changelog

## 2.0.0

### Added
- Zero-dependency TOML configuration (`config/let_your_friend_drink.toml`) powered by `SimpleToml`.
- **Harmful drink protection** (`allowHarmfulDrinks = false` by default): detects `MobEffectCategory.HARMFUL` effects on potions, suspicious stews, consumables, and Ominous Bottles, blocking feeding and notifying the feeder via red actionbar text (`message.let_your_friend_drink.harmful_blocked`).
- **Full-hunger food feeding bridge** (`allowFeedingAtFullHunger = true` by default): safely bridges non-drink food feeding through Let Your Friend Eating even when the recipient's hunger bar is full (`>= 20`), restoring and clamping hunger state afterward.
- **Blacklist support**: `excludedItems` config list (supporting both item IDs and `#tags`) plus built-in `#let_your_friend_drink:excluded` datapack item tag (`tags/items/excluded.json` on 1.20.1, `tags/item/excluded.json` on 1.21.1+).
- Native zero-dependency `FriendDrinkConfigScreen` integrated with Mod Menu (Fabric, compile-only stub with zero runtime leakage), Forge (`ConfigScreenHandler`), and NeoForge (`IConfigScreenFactory`).
- Full 4-language localization: `en_us`, `zh_cn`, `zh_tw`, `ja_jp`.
- Expanded to the full **8-target support matrix**: `1.20.1` (Fabric & Forge), `1.21.1` (Fabric & NeoForge), `26.1.2` (Fabric & NeoForge), and `26.2` (Fabric & NeoForge).
- Dual-package runtime bridge & mixins supporting both Let Your Friend Eating `1.1.x` (`th.in.tamkungz`) and `1.2.x+` (`me.tamkungz`).
- Original 64x64 pixel-art oak badge icon generated by `scripts/draw_icon.py`.

### Changed
- Replaced CurseMaven dependency resolution with automatic Modrinth CDN caching and automated `javap` ABI verification in `scripts/build-matrix.py`.
- Replaced MixinExtras `@WrapMethod` with standard SpongeMixin `@Inject` hooks for universal compatibility across Forge 1.20.1 through 26.2 with zero bundled runtime libraries.

### Removed
- Removed all `RinLib` and `Fabric API` dependencies.
- Removed old AI-generated icon artwork.
